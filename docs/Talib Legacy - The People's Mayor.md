**TALIB AHMED BENSOUDA**

*Lord Mayor, Kanifing Municipal Council — A Record of Delivery, 2018–2026*

Working profile document — compiled from KMC council records and public reporting. Editable draft for website use.

# **Biography**

Talib Ahmed Bensouda was born in 1986 in Bakau, The Gambia. He is the son of Amie Bensouda, a prominent Gambian lawyer and former Attorney General, and Ahmed Bensouda, a former Permanent Secretary at the Ministry of Finance. His paternal grandfather was a Moroccan trader who settled in The Gambia in 1913 and became a close associate of Sir Dawda Kairaba Jawara's father, Almami Jawara.

Bensouda earned a Bachelor of Arts in Economics and Communication Technology from the University of Toronto in 2007\. His early career was spent in sales, management and insurance in Canada and The Gambia, including a role as Marketing Manager at Takaful Gambia Limited. In 2013 he founded Safari Trading, a company producing and marketing hygiene products, before entering local government politics.

In May 2018, at the age of 31, Bensouda was elected Mayor of the Kanifing Municipal Council (KMC), defeating Rambo Jatta of the APRC with 29,325 votes to become the youngest mayor in Gambian history. He was re-elected in May 2023, defeating Bakary Badjie of the ruling National People's Party (NPP). His KMC campaigns were noted for avoiding partisan attacks and focusing on a development-first manifesto under the slogan "Together for a Better KM."

Bensouda has since become a national political figure, serving as National Organizing Secretary of the UDP before emerging as leader of the opposition UNITE Movement for Change (UMC) ahead of the 2026 presidential election, running on a platform built around "The Transformation Agenda." He is married with two children.

# **Environment, Sanitation and Waste Management**

## **The Mbalit Project**

Kanifing's first structured, municipality-wide waste collection system and the first of its kind in the sub-region. Delivered as a three-year, fully funded partnership with QGroup valued at D130 million.

●    24 new compactor trucks procured to introduce organized household waste collection

●    2 skip trucks with 10 skip buckets procured for community dumpsites

●    2 septic emptiers and 3 tipper trucks procured

●    172 youths employed as janitors, drivers, ticket agents and secretaries

●    Council fully paid off the facility and secured ownership of all vehicles

●    500 waste bins distributed to communities, later expanded to 10,000+ under KETP

●    KMC annex property reconstructed, with D5 million invested in internal concrete roads and parking for 50 vehicles

## **Bakoteh Dumpsite — Fencing, Remediation and Recycling**

●    Constructed a 1.8 km, 3.5-metre-high perimeter fence around the Bakoteh dumpsite at a cost of D8 million, fully funded by KMC, to improve site security

●    Engaged University of Wisconsin–Madison engineering researchers (from 2019\) on remediation planning for the 18-hectare former quarry site

●    Identified a site for, and secured EU grant funding toward, a fully engineered sanitary landfill for the Greater Banjul Area, valued at approximately €50 million

●    Installed a tyre recycling plant at the Bakoteh dumpsite (relocated from an original Abuko site plan for cost reasons), reusing an existing German BMZ-funded recycling shed that had been underused; the facility recovers rubber and metal for reuse and is aimed at generating youth and women's employment

●    A further multimillion-dalasi tyre recycling plant was secured in partnership with UNDP, to be housed at the same Bakoteh recycling shed

●    Installed compost production machinery for organic fertiliser at the Bakau Women's Garden (2026), building on an earlier pilot composting and biochar-making programme

## **SoS–BMZ Project (German Government Partnership)**

In partnership with SOS Children's Villages (SoS) and the German Federal Ministry for Economic Cooperation and Development (BMZ), KMC implemented a D42 million "Sustainable Waste Management to Reduce Child Rights Violations at Bakoteh Dumpsite" project. The German government funded the bulk of the project, with KMC contributing a 10% counterpart contribution of D4 million.

●    Construction of two access roads at Bakoteh

●    Creation of a green buffer zone

●    Two sanitary facilities and one open recycling shed constructed

●    Three fire hydrants installed for fire prevention and firefighting

●    35 solar lights installed to improve night-time security and accessibility

●    200 families in Bakoteh, Dippa Kunda and Manjai directly supported

●    A follow-on phase adding a further D30 million in infrastructure was planned

## **Kanifing Environmental Transformation Programme (KETP)**

KETP is KMC's flagship environmental programme, funded by a D180 million (approximately €3 million) European Union grant under the EU's Local Authorities Partnerships for Sustainable Cities 2021 financing mechanism, delivered in partnership with Peterborough City Council (UK) and the Peterborough Environment City Trust (PECT). The programme has three output areas: Waste Management, Education, and Tree Planting & Parks Management.

●    Constructed the Kanifing Municipal Library and Innovation Hub, a D45 million facility contracted to BAJAM Enterprise Limited — foundation laid August 2022, inaugurated December 2024, with a Local Language Section (featuring ADLaN Fula and N'Ko Manding scripts) added in April 2025

●    Provision of 10,000 waste bins under the "one garbage can per household" initiative, with a second phase targeting 25,000 additional bins

●    Installed a KMC community radio station for public education and awareness

●    7 community parks in the procurement process, each planned to include a six-a-side football pitch, outdoor gym, public toilet and basketball court

●    Plan to plant 190,000 trees across the municipality's 19 wards

●    Procurement of a skip truck with 10 skip buckets

●    Recycling infrastructure at Bakoteh dumpsite, including a tyre shredder, glass crusher and weighbridge

●    Construction of 10 community transfer stations for waste storage and sorting

●    A dedicated Career Centre and innovation centre

## **WasteAid UK Partnership**

KMC secured a €100,000 grant in partnership with WasteAid UK and Women's Initiative The Gambia for "Building Climate Resilience through Sustainable Biodegradable Waste Management in Kanifing Municipality," funded through the EU's Global Climate Change Alliance Plus (GCCA+) initiative. Launched July 2021 as a one-year pilot, the project trained women in composting and biochar-making, diverting organic waste from markets to women's gardens in Bakau and Abuko for conversion into compost — with roughly 30 women gardeners directly benefiting.
